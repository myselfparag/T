A small place for your paintings

made by Parag (with love obviously), so this exists even when time doesn't

Dear Tanishka,

I've seen you with a paintbrush in your hand enough times to know it's not just something you do when you're bored. It's the thing that makes sense to you when a lot of other things don't.

I also know you haven't had much time for it lately. Work, life, everything else that fills up a day, it adds up, and painting is usually the first thing that gets pushed to "later." I get it. I'm not going to pretend I have a fix for that.

What I could do is make sure your paintings have a place that isn't just a phone gallery or a folder you forget about. So this is that. No pressure to fill it up fast, no deadline. Just somewhere real for the work you've already done, and space for whatever comes next, whenever next actually happens.

Take your time with it. This page isn't going anywhere.

— Parag

observations

Little things I've noticed

a running list, still growing

You apologise for your paintings before anyone's even seen them, and it's usually still the best thing in the room.

You'll redo one detail four times over something nobody else would notice. That's not perfectionism, that's just care.

You remember colours by where you saw them, not just what they're called.

You get genuinely happy seeing other people's art do well. No competitiveness in it, just respect.

You talk yourself out of showing people your favourite pieces. Those are usually the ones I remember longest.

You notice light and shadow on ordinary days like most people wouldn't bother to.

the artist

Tanishka paints.

Not digital, not filtered, actual paint on actual canvas. Brushes, mixing colours by hand, the kind of work that takes real time and doesn't always cooperate.

Lately that time has been hard to find. This site doesn't fix that. It's just a place that'll be ready whenever she is, holding her paintings the way they deserve to be held: seen, kept, not lost in a camera roll.

About that 2nd above one

You leave the room first when it feels like it's not your turn to be picked. I know why, and I don't love that you've had to learn that habit. But here's the deal: if you're not in the room, I'm not either. And if anything ever tries to make that a competition, I'll handle it, Hulk-mode and all, then hand you the calm explanation after, Einstein-style. You don't have to double check where you stand with me.

About your eyes

I've tried to say this properly a few times and never got it right, so here's the plain version: your eyes give you away before you say a word. When you're proud of something you won't admit to. When you're trying not to laugh at something you shouldn't find funny. When you're annoyed but haven't decided if you're going to say so yet. Most people compliment eyes for how they look. I like yours for what they say.
